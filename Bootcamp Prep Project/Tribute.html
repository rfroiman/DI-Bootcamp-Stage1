The King of Football

Biography

Edson Arantes do Nascimento, known around the world as Pelé, was born on October 23, 1940, in Três Corações, Brazil.

He began his professional career with Santos FC at the age of fifteen and quickly became one of the most talented football players ever seen.

Pelé helped Brazil win three FIFA World Cups (1958, 1962 and 1970), a record that still stands today.

Beyond his incredible achievements on the field, Pelé became a global ambassador for football, inspiring millions of people around the world.

Timeline

  • 1940 – Born in Três Corações, Brazil.
  • 1956 – Joined Santos FC.
  • 1958 – Won his first FIFA World Cup at age 17.
  • 1962 – Won his second FIFA World Cup.
  • 1969 – Santos FC toured Africa, inspiring the famous story of a temporary ceasefire so fans could watch Pelé play.
  • 1970 – Won his third FIFA World Cup.
  • 1975 – Joined the New York Cosmos.
  • 1977 – Retired from professional football.
  • 1999 – Named FIFA Player of the Century.
  • 2022 – Passed away at the age of 82.

Fun Facts

The King of Football

Pelé became known worldwide as "The King of Football" because of his extraordinary talent and achievements.

More Than 1,000 Goals

During his career, Pelé scored over 1,000 goals in official and exhibition matches.

The Match That Stopped a War

During Santos FC's tour of Nigeria in 1969, reports say that a temporary ceasefire occurred so thousands of people could watch Pelé play. Although historians debate some details, the story became one of the greatest legends associated with his career and reflects his enormous worldwide influence.

Young Champion

Pelé was only 17 years old when he won his first FIFA World Cup.
